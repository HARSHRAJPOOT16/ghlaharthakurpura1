Academics

We follow the state board curriculum for Class 9 and Class 10, with a focus on understanding, practice and all-round development.

What we teach

Secondary9 – 10Hindi, English, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Science

A typical day

School timings

  1. 8:00 AMMorning assembly — prayer, news and thought of the day
  2. 8:20 AMLessons begin
  3. 11:00 AMLunch break
  4. 11:30 AMLessons, labs and activities
  5. 2:00 PMSchool closes

Timings may change in summer and winter as per department orders.

Assessment

Exams & progress

Students are assessed throughout the year through class tests, unit tests, half-yearly and annual examinations. Class 10 students appear for the state board examination.

  • Regular unit tests to track progress
  • Remedial and extra classes before board exams
  • Report cards shared at Parent–Teacher Meetings
  • Career guidance for Class 9 and 10 students
Beyond the classroom

Activities & clubs

Every student is encouraged to take part in at least one activity.

🔬

Science club

Experiments, science fairs and exhibitions that bring textbook ideas to life.

🎨

Art & culture

Drawing, rangoli, music, dance and drama for school functions and festivals.

🌳

Eco club

Tree plantation, cleanliness drives and water-saving campaigns in our village.

🏃

Sports

Kabaddi, kho-kho, cricket, volleyball and athletics with inter-school competitions.

📖

Reading corner

Library periods, story-telling and newspaper reading to build a love for books.

🗣️

Debate & quiz

Speaking, quiz and essay competitions that build confidence and general knowledge.
