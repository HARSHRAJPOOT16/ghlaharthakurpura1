Why families choose us

A school built around its students

We follow the state board curriculum and go beyond the textbook with sports, arts, and values that last a lifetime.

📘

Strong academics

Dedicated teachers for Hindi, English, Mathematics, Science, Social Science and Sanskrit, with remedial classes for those who need extra help.

🏏

Sports & fitness

Morning assembly, yoga, kabaddi, kho-kho, cricket and athletics — plus district-level tournaments every year.

💻

Digital learning

A computer lab and smart-class lessons help students build the digital skills they need for the future.

🎓

Scholarships

We help eligible students apply for government scholarships, free textbooks, uniforms and bicycle schemes.

🌱

Values & community

Eco club, NSS-style service activities and celebrations of our festivals keep students connected to their roots.

"Every child who walks through our gate carries the hopes of a family. Our promise is to treat those hopes as our own." — Principal, Government High School
Our vision

Educated, confident and kind citizens

We aim to make quality education accessible to every child of Laharthakurpura, regardless of background, and to prepare them for higher studies, careers and a life of service.
