Admissions

Documents to bring

Admission is free. Please bring originals and one photocopy of each.

  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous school
  • Marksheet of the last class passed
  • Birth certificate
  • Aadhaar card of the student (and parent, if available)
  • Two recent passport-size photographs
  • Caste / income certificate (if applying for scholarships)
