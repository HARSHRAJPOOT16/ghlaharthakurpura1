About our school

Government High School, Laharthakurpura has been a centre of learning for generations of families in our village and the surrounding area.

Who we are

A government school with a family heart

We are a co-educational government high school run under the State Department of School Education. Our school provides free education to students in Class 9 and Class 10 and prepares them for the state board examinations.

Our teachers know each student by name. Many of our alumni have gone on to become teachers, soldiers, engineers, doctors, farmers and leaders in their own communities — and many send their own children back to us.

Our motto

Facilities

Everything a student needs to grow

Our campus is designed to support learning, play and well-being.

  • Spacious classrooms with good light and ventilation
  • Science laboratory for practical work
  • Computer lab and smart-class room
  • Library with textbooks, story books and newspapers
  • Playground for sports and morning assembly
  • Clean drinking water and separate toilets for girls and boys
